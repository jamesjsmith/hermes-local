# Hermes Local

Hermes Local is a personal assistant app that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps its owner keep a calendar, files, notes and to-do lists in order. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

The app connects to the owner's own Google account (Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and read-only Gmail and contacts) to carry out the owner's requests.
